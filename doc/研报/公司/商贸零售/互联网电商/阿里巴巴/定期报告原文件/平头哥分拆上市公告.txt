PROPOSED SPIN-OFF AND SEPARATE LISTING OF T-HEAD ON THE
MAIN BOARD OF THE STOCK EXCHANGE OF HONG KONG LIMITED
Alibaba Group Holding Limited (“Alibaba”, “Alibaba Group” or the “Company”) announced
that it has submitted a proposal in relation to the proposed spin-off and separate listing of T-Head
(Shanghai) Technology Co., Ltd.* (平頭哥(上海)技術股份有限公司) (“T-Head”), a wholly-owned
subsidiary of the Company (the “Proposed Spin-off”) to the Hong Kong Stock Exchange pursuant
to PN15 of the Listing Rules.
The Company is pleased to announce that the Hong Kong Stock Exchange has confirmed that the
Company may proceed with the Proposed Spin-off, and therefore, on September 11, 2026, T-Head,
on a confidential basis, submitted a listing application form (Form A1) to the Hong Kong Stock
Exchange to apply for the listing of, and permission to deal in, the T-Head H Shares on the Main
Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange.
It is currently proposed that the Proposed Spin-off will be effected by way of the Global Offering
of T-Head’s H Shares, comprising (i) a Hong Kong public offering of T-Head’s H Shares for
subscription by the public in Hong Kong; and (ii) a placing of T-Head’s H Shares to institutional
and professional investors. As at the date of this announcement, T-Head is a wholly-owned
subsidiary of the Company and is a leading provider of full-stack data center chips which enable
system-level compute solutions in China. Following completion of the Proposed Spin-off, it is
expected that T-Head will remain as a subsidiary of the Company.

REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF THE PROPOSED SPIN-OFF
The Company considers that the Proposed Spin-off is commercially beneficial to the Company and
T-Head and in the interests of the Shareholders as a whole, for the following reasons:
(a) the Proposed Spin-off should enable investors to better assess and value Alibaba’s Retained
Group business;
(b) the Proposed Spin-off could better reflect the value of the T-Head Group on its own merits,
and investors would be able to appraise and assess the performance and potential of T-Head
separately and distinctly from those of the Retained Group;
(c) T-Head’s business may attract investors seeking dedicated exposure to its business, while the
Retained Group continues to operate a relatively more diversified business model; and
(d) the value of the T-Head Group is expected to be enhanced through the Proposed Spin-off
which will in turn benefit Alibaba and the Shareholders as a whole, given that a listing on the
Hong Kong Stock Exchange will, among other things, (i) enhance T-Head’s standalone profile
among its customers, suppliers and potential strategic partners, which will help it to be in a
better position to negotiate and solicit more business, and (ii) enable T-Head to directly and
independently access both equity and debt capital markets in the future on a standalone basis
should the need arise, as well as further enhance its ability to secure bank credit facilities,
which allows a more efficient deployment of Alibaba Group’s financial resources.
WAIVER FROM STRICT COMPLIANCE WITH PARAGRAPH 3(F) OF PN15
Pursuant to the requirements under paragraph 3(f) of PN15, a listed company contemplating a
spin-off is required to have due regard to the interests of its existing Shareholders by providing
them with an assured entitlement to the shares of the spun-off entity, either by way of a distribution
in specie of existing shares in the spun-off entity or by way of preferred application in any offering
of existing or new shares in the spun-off entity.
The Company has applied for, and the Hong Kong Stock Exchange has granted, a waiver from
strict compliance with the requirement that the Shareholders be provided an assured entitlement to
T-Head’s H Shares (as set out under paragraph 3(f) of PN15) (the “Waiver”) on the following basis
that includes but is not limited to: (a) the extensive endeavor and substantial costs for providing
the assured entitlement by way of distribution in specie or preferred application in any offering
would be disproportionate or excessive to the benefits of the Company’s shareholders conferred by
the assured entitlement, as U.S. securities laws would require T-Head to conduct a public offering
in the United States and thereby require T-Head to file a registration statement with the SEC under
U.S. securities laws, among other procedures, that would be unduly burdensome; (b) a distribution
in specie of T-Head’s H Shares would be unduly burdensome and impracticable due to the PRC
legal and operational restrictions applicable to T-Head as a company established under the laws
of the PRC and the Company as a dual listed company, including but not limited to the lock-up
on existing shares issued prior to the Global Offering under the PRC laws; (c) it would be unduly
burdensome and not practically necessary to seek Shareholders’ approval to waive the assured
entitlement as set out in paragraph 3(f) of PN15; and (d) the Proposed Spin-off is insignificant to
the Company and its Shareholders with reference to the Company’s total assets, revenue and net
income.

IMPLICATIONS UNDER THE LISTING RULES
Details in respect of the Proposed Spin-off, including the size and structure of the Global Offering
and the extent of the decrease in the Company’s shareholding percentage in T-Head, have not yet
been finalized.
As it is expected that the highest of the applicable percentage ratios (as defined in the Listing
Rules) in respect of the Proposed Spin-off would be less than 5%, the Proposed Spin-off, if it
proceeds, would not constitute a discloseable transaction for the Company under the Listing Rules.
It is also expected that the Proposed Spin-off, if materializes, will not affect the Company’s dual
primary listing status on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange and the NYSE.
The Company will comply with the applicable requirements under the Listing Rules and make
further announcement(s) in relation to the Proposed Spin-off as and when appropriate.
The Proposed Spin-off is subject to, among other things, the obtaining of approvals from the
Listing Committee of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange for the listing of, and permission to
deal in, T-Head’s H Shares, the completion of the filing with the China Securities Regulatory
Commission for the listing and offering of T-Head’s H Shares, and the final decisions of the
Company and T-Head. There is no assurance that the Proposed Spin-off will take place or
as to when it may take place. Shareholders and potential investors of the Company should
therefore exercise caution when dealing in or investing in the securities of the Company.
